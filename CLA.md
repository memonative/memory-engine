# Memonative Contributor License Agreement

## In plain English

This is the short version. The agreement below is what actually counts, but
here is what it means:

- **You keep your copyright.** You are not selling or giving away your code.
- **You give Memonative permission to use it however it needs**, including in
  the paid hosted service, and including under a different licence later.
- **You confirm the code is yours to give** — not copied from somewhere else,
  and not something your employer owns.
- **You promise nothing.** No warranty, no support obligation, no liability.

Memonative is open-core: the engine is source-available under BSL 1.1 and also
runs as a commercial hosted service. That licence stops anyone — including us,
for code we do not own — from running the project as a managed service. Without
this agreement, a contribution could not be used in the hosted build, which
means it could not be merged at all. That is the only reason this document
exists.

You sign once. It covers every contribution you ever make here.
